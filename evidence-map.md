# Portfolio Evidence Map

This map shows how the reusable patterns connect back to the three case-study repositories.

The purpose is not to claim that every project used identical implementation details. The purpose is to show where the same underlying design logic appears across different kinds of work.

| Pattern | Workflow Transformation | Community Operations Automation | Small Business Growth Diagnostic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human Review Gate | Human approval before decision-ready synthesis | Review path for exceptions, sensitive cases, and commitments | Leadership validation before strategy/testing |
| Evidence Before Synthesis | Claims traced to sources before synthesis | Known information and established rules before routine response | Baseline and evidence definition before diagnosis |
| Keep Unknowns Visible | Conflicts, missing evidence, and qualifiers stay explicit | Ambiguous requests are not forced into routine handling | Proxies, scenarios, incomplete history, and measurement gaps stay visible |
| Structured Input → Structured Output | Source material becomes structured extraction and classified evidence | Requests become categorized cases with routing and follow-up | Operating data becomes baseline, capacity, retention, and measurement-gap views |
| Fail Toward Review | Unsupported or conflicting claims return to validation | Unknown or higher-consequence cases escalate | Incomplete evidence becomes a limitation or open question rather than a forced conclusion |
| Diagnose Before Intervention | Research is staged before decision support | Classification and routing happen before action | Constraints are tested before tactics are recommended |

## What changes by context

The implementation changes because the work changes.

A research workflow needs evidence traceability. An operations workflow needs routing and escalation rules. A business diagnostic needs clear definitions, scenario boundaries, and leadership validation.

The reusable part is the logic:

1. structure the problem
2. make the evidence or rules inspectable
3. separate stages that require different kinds of reasoning
4. preserve uncertainty
5. stop for human judgment when the workflow reaches its boundary
6. only then move toward action
